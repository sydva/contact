Privacy policy

Calendar Busy Sync is a personal tool used only by Sydney Von Arx on Sydney's own Google accounts.

  • What it accesses: event times on the connected Google calendars, through the Google Calendar API.
  • What it does with it: creates, updates and deletes "Busy" placeholder events on the other connected calendar. Placeholders carry no titles, attendees or descriptions from the original events.
  • What it stores: only the OAuth tokens needed to keep the sync running, held in encrypted cloud secret storage. Event data is not kept, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Revoking access: remove "Calendar Busy Sync" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
